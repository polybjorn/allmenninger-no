My name is Lene Røkke Mathisen, and I live on Vibrandsøy, with the sea, the sheep and the heathland as my nearest neighbours. That shapes how I work: I am drawn to projects that grow out of local conditions, where people know the place and its nature well, and where people and nature get to work together rather than against one another.

I am an environmental scientist by training, with a master's degree in Water Resources and Coastal Management from the University of Bergen and a bachelor's degree in Environmental Science from the University of Leeds. For the past 20 years I have worked as an adviser and coordinator on climate and environmental issues, managing projects large and small in mapping, analysis and physical measures.

I have solid experience with outreach assignments aimed at a range of audiences, and with running events. In 2025 I founded Allmenninger to work more deliberately on projects in sustainable transition – through outreach, the facilitation of dialogue workshops and courses, and advisory work on climate adaptation at the local level.

Previously I have worked as environmental adviser and head of agriculture in Haugesund municipality, senior adviser for planning and management at the Norwegian Coastal Administration West, adviser at the Norwegian Agriculture Agency, and adviser at Forsvarsbygg Futura.
